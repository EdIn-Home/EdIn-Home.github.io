Abstract

This study investigates the integration of artificial intelligence tools in academic research, examining both the extent and the nature of AI contributions across different sections of scholarly publications. Using a novel section-level attribution framework, we analyzed 1,240 peer-reviewed papers spanning five disciplines to assess how AI assistance manifests in introduction, methods, results, discussion, and conclusion sections. Our findings reveal significant variation in AI utilization patterns both within and across disciplines, with methodological sections showing the highest rates of AI assistance. We propose a standardized reporting framework for AI contribution disclosure and evaluate its potential to improve research reproducibility and transparency in the scientific literature.

About EdIn

EdIn is the next-generation academic indexing platform built for an era where AI is reshaping how science is produced, reported, and evaluated.

🔍
AI Transparency

Every indexed paper receives a section-level AI contribution analysis, distinguishing between human-written, AI-assisted, and AI-generated content.

⚗️
Reproducibility

Our reproducibility scoring evaluates data availability, code sharing, and methodological transparency to support open science.

✅
Integrity Scoring

Integrity scores reflect disclosure completeness, citation accuracy, and compliance with AI usage reporting standards from major publishers.

🌍
Global Coverage

With 4.28M papers across 18,420 journals and 2.9M authors, EdIn covers research from every continent and discipline.
