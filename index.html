AI Transparency

Every indexed paper receives a section-level AI contribution analysis distinguishing human-written, AI-assisted, and AI-generated content using validated detection methodology.

⚗️
Reproducibility

Reproducibility scoring evaluates data availability, code sharing, and methodological transparency to support open science and reduce the replication crisis.

✅
Integrity Scoring

Integrity scores reflect disclosure completeness, citation accuracy, and compliance with AI usage reporting standards adopted by Nature, Elsevier, and PLOS.

🌍
Global Coverage

With 4.28M papers across 18,420 journals and 2.9M authors from 190+ countries, EdIn covers all disciplines with particular depth in Africa and the Global South.

API Integration

Integrate EdIn's transparency metrics directly into your institution's research management system, journal submission workflows, or peer review platforms via our REST API.

Authorization: Bearer <your-api-key>

# Bulk institutional export
